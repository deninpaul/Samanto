About us

Established in 2019, SAMANTO ASSOCIATES PVT. LTD. is a multidisciplinary consultancy delivering innovative, cost-effective solutions across civil engineering, project management, cost consultancy and property valuation.

Navigating project development and execution involves complex risks and significant financial stakes. Our technical solutions help projects meet quality, time and budget goals through a client-centric, collaborative approach.

As a Registered IBBI Valuer Organisation, we provide valuations for banks, financial institutions, corporate and public assets, wealth tax assessments, and detailed Bill of Quantity valuation for statutory authorities.
