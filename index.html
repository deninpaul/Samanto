About us

Rooted in
innovation.
Driven by value.

Established in 2019, SAMANTO ASSOCIATES PVT. LTD. is a multidisciplinary consultancy delivering innovative, cost-effective solutions across civil engineering, project management, cost consultancy and property valuation. Navigating project development and execution involves complex risks and significant financial stakes.

Our innovative and cost-effective technical solutions ensure projects are executed with quality, time, and budget. We ensure a client-centric and collaborative approach with clients, partners and specialists for seamless project delivery.

As a Registered IBBI Valuer Organisation, we provide comprehensive valuation services for banks, financial institutions, corporate and public assets, wealth tax assessments, and detailed Bill of Quantity valuation for statutory authorities.

Property Valuation

Managed by Sam and Anto

Reliable valuations are essential for informed financial decisions and asset assessments. As an IBBI-registered Valuer Organisation, we deliver valuations across residential, commercial, institutional and industrial properties.

Our data-driven, IVS-compliant reports combine local market intelligence, property analysis and regulatory expertise, ensuring accuracy, defensibility, professional ethics and confidentiality.

Our project management services bring architects, engineers and contractors together under focused leadership. Through strategic planning, proactive coordination and rigorous cost control, we drive efficient project execution within defined timelines and budgets.

Leveraging industry best practices and modern management tools, we align project delivery with client objectives while maintaining quality.

Managed by Resma and Greety

Our studio transforms ideas into practical, sustainable and high-performing projects through innovative design and cost-effective engineering solutions. Cost Management

Managed by Anto and Adhithyan

Effective cost management is essential for financially sustainable project delivery. Our cost consultancy services provide financial oversight throughout the project lifecycle, from feasibility and concept design to execution.

We offer budgeting, quantity surveying, detailed estimation, value engineering, whole-life costing, tendering, contract administration and on-site cost monitoring to optimize expenditure and project value.
